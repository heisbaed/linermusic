Cine music Mobile and Cine music TV 0.1.8

- Separate mobile and Android TV APKs, dedicated interfaces and platform-specific update feeds. Existing installation identities and signing are preserved.
- Shared playback devices, phone-to-TV handoff, remote playback controls and offline phone listening with reconnection.
- Mobile Connect replaces the former top-bar Lyrics button. Lyrics remain accessible from the visible preview. Artwork, seeking and transport positions are preserved.
- TV retains its player tabs and adds a slimmer mini-player with favorite, shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat and queue controls.
- Unboxed animated pink C-and-note splash, Cine music wordmark and By Osborne's Digital Solutions branding. Startup closes when the app is ready.
- Includes v0.1.7 discovery, lyrics, sorting and browser-sharing improvements.

- Persistent artwork caching across app sessions, bounded to 64 MB on mobile and 32 MB on TV.
- TV Smart Shuffle cycles Off, Shuffle and Smart Shuffle in both players.

Final testing was stopped at the owner's request. The latest caching and Smart Shuffle changes remain unverified on devices. Earlier verification and remaining limitations are recorded in PROGRESS.md. Song-specific social previews remain skipped at the owner's request. Existing debug signing is preserved for update compatibility. Android requires installer confirmation for APK updates.
