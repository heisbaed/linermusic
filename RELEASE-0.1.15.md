# Cine music 0.1.15

Version code 18. Separate Mobile and TV APKs.

- Daily Mix rotates recommendation seeds by local day and refresh counter, excludes recent mix recordings, limits repeated artists, and tracks refresh history. The date rolls over while the app remains open. A new daily mix is prepared without opening another page. If no fresh music is available, saved mixes stay unchanged.
- Search retains catalog/video results without album IDs or recognized music-video classifications. It preserves the source catalog ranking, merges typed pages in provider order, and removes duplicate IDs; local metadata rules no longer discard or rerank search results. The reports for Gidi and Katika motivated this change; their live availability was not verified.
- Recommendations stay anchored to the selected song. Older listening seeds and unrelated favorite artists no longer steer radio/autoplay. Cached genre metadata rejects known genre mismatches; unknown metadata remains eligible from source radio. TV track selection preserves the full queue for Previous.
- Genre chips use exact recording metadata from the iTunes catalog, with matched MusicBrainz recording/artist genres as fallback. Artist fallback is broader than song-level classification. Title words no longer invent music genres; explicit version labels such as Live remain separate. Background indexing is cached, rate-limited and independent of playback. Manual labels remain available. Categories reflect actual available metadata, with no fixed limit of three or four chips. Unknown songs stay unclassified.
- Mobile Liked Songs uses flat rows, restrained filled hearts, compact search/sort controls and scrollable genre chips. Library has a direct Settings shortcut on mobile only.
- TV browsing has denser rows, track numbers/durations, a two-column detail view and refreshed home shortcuts from actual library content. Glass effects are concentrated on navigation and playback surfaces. Full-screen artwork mode uses higher-resolution cover imagery with a readable control panel; split lyrics/queue views remain available.
- Mobile widget has compact one-row and expanded layouts, with minimum resize dimensions of 150 x 64 dp. Previous/Next controls adapt to available width; Play remains available. A rounded translucent panel, cropped artwork, vector controls and thin progress bar replace the old design. Widget/media artwork loads through the shared persistent artwork cache rather than relying solely on memory. Failed/ended tracks reopen when Play is pressed. The redundant Stop control was removed.

- Likes and playlist additions now store actual added timestamps. Date-added sorting offers Newest first and Oldest first; cloud key ordering no longer decides the position of new saves. Historical entries without dates keep their available saved order instead of receiving invented historical timestamps.

## Evidence and limits

Reviewed the owner's two supplied phone screenshots and opened their Spotify Liked Songs view as explicitly requested. Consulted Spotify's public TV/genre-filter references and official metadata API documentation. Compiled both interfaces and APKs. No automated tests or new-build playback/UI checks were performed, respecting the testing freeze. Visual parity, exact named-track results, genre coverage, recommendation quality and widget rendering remain unverified on the new build. Genre enrichment requires connectivity and may take several minutes for a large library; incomplete catalog metadata does not become a guessed classification.
